# Team workspaces

## Product Contract

### Problem Frame

Solo users churn after 30 days because the tool has nothing to share. Teams of 3-10 are the segment most likely to convert to paid, so shared workspaces should come before any other roadmap item.

### Requirements

- R1. A user can create a workspace and invite others by email.
- R2. Notes in a workspace are visible to every member.
- R3. Members can leave; the creator can remove members.
- R4. Workspaces ship before the mobile app.

### Success Criteria

- Paid conversion among new signups rises from 2% to 5% within one quarter of launch.

### Scope Boundaries

- Deferred: per-note permissions, roles beyond creator and member.
